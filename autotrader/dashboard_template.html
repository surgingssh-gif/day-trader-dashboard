How to read this dashboard ▾
The strategy
At 9:45 AM New York time, note the high and low of the first 15 minutes (the opening range). Buy when a 5-minute candle closes above the high. Entries stop at 3:30 PM.
Stop and target
Set before the entry. The stop sits at the middle of the opening range; the target is the same distance above the entry. Anything still open is closed at 3:55 PM.
R (risk unit)
1R is what you lose if the stop is hit. +1R means you made as much as you risked. Dollar figures assume risked per trade.
Profit factor
Money won on winning trades divided by money lost on losing ones. Above 1.0 makes money; 1.3+ over many trades is a solid edge.
Drawdown
The biggest drop from a high point in the running total. It shows the worst losing stretch you'd have had to sit through.
Time Machine
Replays the rest of the day 10,000 times using the stock's own last ~60 sessions, never the day itself, so the odds are made before the outcome is known.
Watchlist vs scanner
Watchlist stocks are checked every day. Scanner picks are the day's 5 most-traded stocks that broke out by 10:15 AM.
How many trades is enough?
Results swing a lot by luck. Under 30 trades means very little; around 100 starts to show whether an edge is real.
What this isn't
Paper results from free, sometimes delayed 5-minute data. Real fills, fees and slippage are worse. Not financial advice.
